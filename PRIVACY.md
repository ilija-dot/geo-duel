# Geo Duel – Privacy note

Geo Duel is an educational geography game for students. We only collect what the game needs to work.
(The same note is in the game: **Account → Privacy**, in English and Macedonian.)

## What we store
- **Email and password**, only if you create an account. Your email is used only to log in, so you
  don't lose your game across browsers and devices. The password is stored encrypted; nobody can read it.
- Your **nickname, player ID and avatar**.
- Your **duels**: the questions, the answers you picked and the points.
- **Levels you create**, and reports of inappropriate levels.
- Your **coins, gems and bought items**, so they follow you to every device.
- **On your device only**: language and your friends list.

## Who sees what
Other players only see your nickname, avatar, player ID and wins (in duels and on the leaderboard).
Nobody sees your email. The game has no real names, photos or chat.

## What we don't do
No ads, no tracking, no analytics. We don't sell or share your data, and we don't send marketing emails.

## Where it's stored
Online data is stored with [Supabase](https://supabase.com), on servers in the EU (Ireland).

## Deleting your data
In the game, open **Account → Delete my account**. This immediately deletes your account, email,
duels and levels from the server.

## If you're under 16
Ask a parent or guardian before creating an account with your email. You can also play as a guest
without an email.
